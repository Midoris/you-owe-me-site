# Content inventory and anti-cannibalization guardrails

Every proposed page must be compared with this inventory. Similarity of page type is not a reason to reject an idea; overlap in user job, query family, lifecycle stage, promised output, and canonical answer is.

## Required overlap test for every proposed page

1. Name the exact user job in one sentence.
2. Name the lifecycle stage: before money moves, recording, splitting, repayment planning, partial repayment, reminder, dispute/escalation, confirmation/receipt, or long-term maintenance.
3. Identify the closest three existing pages and explain why none already satisfies the same job.
4. Distinguish the new page by at least two substantive dimensions: audience/situation, input, output, decision, interaction, evidence, or product capability. Wording alone is insufficient.
5. If the intent belongs inside an existing page, recommend expansion instead of a new URL.
6. Reject ideas that merely substitute ‘friend/family/roommate/partner’ into an existing template without distinct observed demand and a distinct answer.
7. Protect the established winner URLs unless there is strong evidence for a controlled change.
