# You Owe Me organic-authority research pack

Prepared: 2026-08-10  
Search Console window: 2026-05-08 through 2026-08-07 (Web search)  
Property: `sc-domain:you-owe-me.com`

## Purpose

This pack is designed for GPT-5.6 Sol Pro to investigate the deeper reasons certain You Owe Me pages succeed, compare them with similar-format pages that do not, and propose only high-confidence new pages that can expand product awareness and topical authority in Google Search and AI answer systems.

The central instruction is **not** to explain success by page type. The site already contains many guides, tools, and solution pages. The analysis must look beneath those containers at problem framing, user mental models, exact language, intent breadth, urgency, lifecycle stage, emotional stakes, functional completeness, title/snippet alignment, topical/internal authority, product differentiation, and competition.

## Important Search Console limitations

- Search Console suppresses/anonymizes many queries. Page-filtered query rows do **not** add up to page totals, especially for tools. Absence from a query export is not absence of demand.
- Page and property aggregates use different aggregation rules; do not sum page rows and expect exact property totals.
- Average position is an impression-weighted aggregate across queries, devices, countries, and dates—not a literal rank.
- High CTR on a small sample is not comparable with CTR on tens of thousands of impressions.
- The workbook includes the most recent 28 days versus the preceding 28 days inside the 3-month window. Small pages can show extreme growth percentages from tiny bases.
- Search Console measures search acquisition, not business value. App Store opens, installations, paid conversion, retention, and product usage are not included.
- The Reddit section is qualitative language research, not search-volume evidence.

## Winner/cohort selection

The 13 page-filtered dossiers cover:

- the largest pages by clicks;
- the largest pages by impressions;
- high CTR among pages with at least 500 impressions;
- strong-position/low-CTR pages that may reveal intent or snippet mismatch;
- emerging pages with rapid recent visibility growth;
- comparable narrow utilities and situation-specific pages useful as counterexamples.

This is intentionally a mixed cohort. GPT-5.6 Pro should create its own success taxonomy and may decide that some pages are demand validators or emerging opportunities rather than true winners.
