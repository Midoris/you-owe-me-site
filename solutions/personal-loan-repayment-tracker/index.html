Personal loan repayment tracker for people you know

Track a Personal Loan and Keep the Repayment Plan Current

Create a named loan, choose a realistic way to repay it, and record what actually happens. You Owe Me keeps the remaining balance, next payment, reminder, and projected payoff current after partial, extra, early, or late payments—whether you lent the money or borrowed it.

For personal loans between friends, family members, partners, or roommates—not bank loans or automatic payment collection.

Repaying a bank, credit union, vehicle-finance provider, or another formal lender? Use the Debt Repayment Tracker for a manual working record that is reconciled with official lender statements.

Is this the kind of loan you need to track?

This page is for one identifiable personal loan that will be repaid over time. The arrangement can be informal, but repayment should already be understood.

Use a simpler route when…

  • Repayment was never clearly agreed. Clarify whether it was a gift or a loan first.
  • You only need to divide one amount into installments once. Create a one-time repayment plan.
  • Expenses and repayments moved both ways across the whole relationship. Reconstruct a mixed running balance.
  • Several known repayments already happened and you only need the current remainder. Calculate what remains after known payments.

A payment is a real entry. An installment is a projected row in the schedule. Keeping those separate is what lets the history stay honest when the future plan changes.

The loan and the plan use the same record

Every repayment is saved as a real update to the loan. The remaining loan balance and the repayment-plan progress therefore come from the same financial history—not two separate numbers that can silently drift apart.

Record the real payment

Add a payment from the plan, the loan, or the normal entry flow after selecting that loan. The real entry updates what remains.

Reconcile corrections

If a relevant payment is edited or deleted, the app rebuilds the plan from the updated loan history instead of leaving stale progress behind.

Preserve what happened

Changing or ending a plan does not delete the loan or its real payments. Earlier plan versions and completed history remain understandable.

History stays honest. The future stays current.

A reminder that follows the real next payment

The plan reminder is not a repeating alert that keeps using the original amount forever. After a partial, full, or extra payment, You Owe Me updates the reminder to the next unpaid installment, its current remaining amount, and the correct due date.

Already partway through the loan? Start from what is true today

You do not need to invent every earlier transaction or rebuild the arrangement from memory. Begin with the amount that is definitely still owed now.

See the loan inside the whole financial relationship

The loan detail answers what is happening with this particular loan. The Timeline shows what has happened between you and the person—and what may need attention now.

The Timeline can bring together:

  • A named loan starting or settling
  • A repayment plan starting, changing, or ending
  • A real payment made or received
  • The next payment, a payment due today, or an overdue plan
  • A follow-up or repayment update
  • A shared statement, PDF, or other record
  • A period when everything is settled

A plan-linked payment keeps its loan name and amount instead of appearing as an unexplained transaction. When something needs attention, the Timeline can open the exact plan or the appropriate communication action.

When no action is needed, the Timeline can say that too. Guidance should reduce uncertainty, not create pressure for its own sake.

Share the current understanding clearly

Create a focused PDF loan record and choose whether to include the active or completed repayment-plan summary.

The PDF can include:

  • The loan name and people involved
  • The initial amount or initial tracked balance
  • The remaining balance
  • Who owes whom
  • Payment and update history
  • The regular payment and frequency
  • The next payment amount and date
  • The current projected payoff
  • Relevant interest and plan status

Use it to confirm the current understanding, keep a personal copy, or share clearer context than a screenshot or chat message.

The PDF is a shareable loan record. It is not a binding contract or a regulated bank statement.

A record and planning tool—not a lender or payment service

You Owe Me helps one person keep a clear record, plan repayments, follow progress, and communicate the current understanding.

You Owe Me can

  • Track a named personal loan
  • Record payments and additional borrowing
  • Compare and schedule repayment options
  • Recalculate plan progress after real changes
  • Remind the user about the next relevant payment
  • Show the loan inside the wider Timeline
  • Share a focused loan record

It does not

  • Lend or underwrite money
  • Move, withdraw, or collect payments
  • Connect to a bank to detect transactions
  • Enforce repayment
  • Replace a legal agreement
  • Guarantee that another person will pay
  • Produce a regulated bank statement
  • Create a shared two-party repayment contract through Balance Sync

The app records and plans. The people involved still make the decisions and payments themselves.
