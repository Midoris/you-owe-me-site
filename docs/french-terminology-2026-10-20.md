## Voice and typography

Use professional, approachable **vous**, active verbs and natural French across French-speaking markets. Avoid France-only assumptions, banking terminology where a simple personal record is meant, and promises of payment collection or financial advice. First-person situation labels (« Quelqu’un me doit de l’argent ») describe the visitor’s need; the rest of the page addresses them with vous. Genuine quoted reviews retain first person and may quote an informal conversation; they are labeled translations of English App Store reviews and preserve attribution.

Use typographic apostrophes, accents on capitals and French guillemets. Place nonbreaking spaces before `: ; ! ? %` and inside guillemets, and between monetary values and their explicit currency codes. Month names are lowercase in running prose. Human-facing decimals use commas and grouped values use nonbreaking spaces. Currency remains explicit: source USD examples stay USD; language does not convert amounts, choose EUR or select a national storefront. The raw native financial schema, parsing contracts and transfer values stay separate from presentation.
