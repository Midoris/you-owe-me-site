Personal loan payment tracker

Record Actual Loan Payments and See What Is Still Owed

Track what actually happened after a personal loan repayment plan began. Add the amount currently owed, the agreed repayment rhythm, and each real payment to see what remains, which installment is partly or fully covered, what is due next, and how the projected payoff has changed.

Your entries stay in this browser unless you choose to save them on this device. No sign-up is required.

For personal loans between people you know. This tool does not lend money, move payments, create a legal agreement, or enforce repayment.

Not sure what belongs in the record? Read how to track a personal loan between friends or family before entering the current balance and payments.

How this tracker applies payments

These are the calculation rules used on this page. They are recordkeeping rules for this browser tool, not legal instructions.

How the tracker handles common payment events.
What happensHow the tracker handles it
A payment matches the installmentMarks that installment paid and moves to the next unpaid amount.
A payment is smallerLeaves the unpaid part of the oldest installment visible.
A payment is largerCompletes the oldest unpaid amount and applies the excess forward.
A payment is earlyRecords the real date and applies the money to the oldest unpaid scheduled amount.
A payment is latePreserves the original due date and shows the amount as overdue until it is covered.
More money is borrowedIncreases the remaining balance and extends the future projection without rewriting paid history.
An entry is corrected or deletedRecalculates every later balance and installment allocation.
The loan is paid off earlyMarks future installments that are no longer required as no longer needed.
  • Activity is processed in date order. Entries on the same date are processed in the order they were added.
  • Payments apply to the oldest unpaid scheduled amount first.
  • Paid history remains historical. Future projections may change.
  • The final installment may be smaller than the regular payment.
  • Paid before this tracker is context only and is not subtracted from the current balance again.
  • Amounts are calculated in the currency’s smallest supported unit rather than with floating-point arithmetic.

For monthly plans, the tracker keeps the original day of the month when possible. When a month is shorter, it uses that month’s final day.

See a partial payment, an extra payment, and more borrowing

In the worked example, a friend owes 1,200 and the regular plan is 200. A payment of 150 leaves 50 open. A later payment of 300 completes that 50, covers the next 200, and applies 50 forward. Another 100 is then added to the loan, so the future projection extends without changing the payments already recorded.

Is this browser tracker enough?

Use the lightest method that will stay accurate. A browser snapshot is useful when the situation is simple. An ongoing app record becomes useful when the loan will keep changing.

  • No future changes expected
  • Both people already agree
  • No lasting history required

  • Timeline and PDF history matter

Questions about the browser tracker

Does this tool create a repayment plan?

No. It tracks an existing regular plan. Use the Payment Plan Calculator if the payment amount or payoff timing has not been decided.

What if there is no regular repayment plan?

You can still record payments, additional borrowing, and the current remaining balance. Without a regular plan, the tracker cannot show ahead, overdue, next-payment, or projected-payoff states.

What happens when a payment is smaller or larger than planned?

A smaller payment leaves part of the oldest installment open. A larger payment completes the oldest unpaid amount and applies the excess forward.

What if more money is borrowed later?

Record it as additional borrowing. The remaining balance increases and the future projection extends, while payments already recorded stay in the history.

Does this page save my information?

By default, the tracker remains only in the current tab. If you select “Save this tracker on this device,” the page stores one local copy in this browser. It is not synced to You Owe Me, cannot be imported automatically, and may disappear if browser data is cleared.

Is this a legal loan record or payment service?

No. This tool does not lend money, move payments, collect money, enforce repayment, provide legal or tax advice, or create a binding agreement.

Can both people edit the browser record?

No. This browser tool is a one-person record. You can copy, print, or export the current snapshot, but it does not provide shared editing or create a two-party agreement.
